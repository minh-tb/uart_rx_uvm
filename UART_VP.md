| 1.1 | verify rx_data matching with o_data | Random 8-Bit Payload Transfer (Analogous to `mux_demo_test` `ref_pkt` vs `act_pkt`) | Dynamic Check (Scoreboard) | `uart_demo_seq` randomizes 8-bit `rx_data` packets. `uart_driver` frames `{stop=1'b1, data[7:0], start=1'b0}` LSB-first on `uart_if.rx_data` at 115200 baud (87 `clk` cycles/bit at 10 MHz). | `receiver.v` samples start bit at mid-bit (`clk` 43) and each of the 8 data bits every 87 `clk` cycles into `r_Rx_Byte[7:0]`, outputting `o_data` when `o_valid=1` in `s_CLEANUP`. | `tb/chk/sb.sv` (`exp_imp` from `uart_driver.ap` vs `act_imp` from `uart_slave_monitor.ap`) | 100% match between `ref_pkt.data` (`rx_data`) and `act_pkt.data` (`o_data`); `UVM_ERROR == 0`. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 1.2 | verify rx_data matching with o_data | Directed Boundary & Alternating Data Patterns (`0x00`, `0xFF`, `0x55`, `0xAA`) | Dynamic Check + Functional Coverage | Transmit all-0s (`8'h00`, same level as start bit), all-1s (`8'hFF`, same level as stop/idle bit), and max-toggle alternating patterns (`8'h55`, `8'hAA`) LSB-first. | DUT accurately distinguishes start/stop framing bits from payload bits and samples all 8 bit indices (`r_Bit_Index 0..7`) without bit slip or phase shift. | `tb/chk/sb.sv` data comparison + `rx_data_cov` covergroup bins | `o_data` matches `8'h00`, `8'hFF`, `8'h55`, `8'hAA`; covergroup bins hit in `top.ucdb` (`rx_data_cov.log`). | `uart_demo_test` |
| 1.3 | verify rx_data matching with o_data | Multi-Packet Back-to-Back Streaming & Queue Drain Check (Analogous to `mux_in_sb` `check_phase`) | Dynamic Check (`check_phase`) | Send multiple consecutive UART frames in `uart_demo_seq` and wait for drain time at end of `run_phase`. | Every transmitted frame is deserialized in FIFO order with no dropped, duplicated, or leftover packets in scoreboard queue. | `tb/chk/sb.sv` `check_phase` (verifies `exp_queue.size() == 0` and `match_count == num_pkts`) | `tx_cnt == rx_cnt`; `exp_queue` empty at `check_phase`; `UVM_ERROR == 0`. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 1.4 | verify rx_data matching with o_data | Error Injection / Negative Verification (Analogous to `mux_cfg.inject_error`) | Negative / Error Injection Check | Enable error injection in `uart_cfg` (`inject_error = 1`) or corrupt driven/monitored data byte in `uart_err_test`. | Scoreboard detects mismatch between expected reference byte (`rx_data`) and actual received byte (`o_data`). | `tb/cfg/uart_cfg.sv` (`inject_error`) + `tb/chk/sb.sv` mismatch detection | Scoreboard accurately flags data mismatch when error is injected. | `uart_err_test` |
| 1.5 | verify rx_data matching with o_data | Functional Coverage of Transmitted `rx_data` (Analogous to `mux_in_cov.sv`) | Functional Coverage (`covergroup`) | Sample 8-bit `rx_data` transactions sent across simulation via `rx_data_cov` covergroup during coverage run (`make coverage`). | Covergroup records sampled `rx_data` values into `top.ucdb` for coverage reporting (`vcover dump top.ucdb \| tee rx_data_cov.log`). | `rx_data_cov` covergroup sampling `pkt.data` (saved to `top.ucdb`) | `top.ucdb` generated with 0 errors and non-zero coverage bins dumped in `rx_data_cov.log`. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 2.1 | verify o_valid is stable | Single-Clock Pulse Width in `s_CLEANUP` (Analogous to `mux.sv` SVA `a3` timing check) | Assertion (SVA) + Monitor Check | Drive complete 10-bit UART frame (start + 8 data + stop) and observe `o_valid` (`o_Rx_DV`) on `posedge clk`. | `o_valid` asserts HIGH (`1'b1`) at the end of `s_RX_STOP_BIT` for exactly 1 `clk` cycle (`s_CLEANUP`) and deasserts to `1'b0` on the very next `clk` edge (`s_IDLE`). | SVA `p_valid_one_clk`: `@(posedge clk) disable iff (rst) $rose(o_valid) \|=> !o_valid;` | `o_valid` pulse width is strictly 1 `clk` cycle (100 ns) per frame; 0 SVA failures. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 2.2 | verify o_valid is stable | Low Stability During Idle & Active Bit Reception (Analogous to `mux.sv` SVA `a1`/`a2` state checks) | Assertion (SVA) | Monitor `o_valid` across `s_IDLE` (`rx_data=1`), `s_RX_START_BIT` (43 clks), `s_RX_DATA_BITS` (8 x 87 clks), and `s_RX_STOP_BIT` (87 clks). | `o_valid` remains stable LOW (`1'b0`) with zero glitches or premature assertions whenever `r_SM_Main != s_CLEANUP` (`3'b100`). | SVA `p_valid_low_in_rx`: `@(posedge clk) disable iff (rst) (r_SM_Main != 3'b100) \|-> (o_valid == 1'b0);` | `o_valid == 1'b0` for all non-cleanup states; 0 SVA failures. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 2.3 | verify o_valid is stable | No Unknown (`X`/`Z`) Glitch on `o_valid` & `o_data` After Reset | Assertion (SVA) | Observe `o_valid` across reset release, idle line, and active serial reception on every `posedge clk`. | `o_valid` never transitions to `X` or `Z` (`!$isunknown(o_valid)`), and when `o_valid == 1`, `o_data` is known (`!$isunknown(o_data)`). | SVA `a_valid_known`: `@(posedge clk) disable iff (rst) !$isunknown(o_valid);`<br>SVA `a_data_known_on_valid`: `@(posedge clk) disable iff (rst) o_valid \|-> !$isunknown(o_data);` | 0 unknown (`X`/`Z`) states on `o_valid` or valid `o_data` throughout simulation. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 2.4 | verify o_valid is stable | `o_data` Hold Stability During `o_valid` Assertion Window | Assertion (SVA) + Monitor Sampling | Transmit random 8-bit frames; monitor `o_data` (`o_Rx_Byte`) at the clock edge where `o_valid == 1'b1`. | `r_Rx_Byte` is latched on the last data bit (`s_RX_DATA_BITS`) 87 clocks before `o_valid` asserts, so `o_data` is completely stable (`$stable(o_data)`) while `o_valid` is HIGH. | SVA `p_data_stable_on_valid`: `@(posedge clk) disable iff (rst) o_valid \|-> $stable(o_data);` | `o_data` holds stable throughout the `o_valid=1` cycle; monitor samples clean byte. | `uart_demo_test` |
| 2.5 | verify o_valid is stable | False Start-Bit Glitch Rejection (Stop/Start Framing Stability) | Assertion (SVA) + Protocol Check | Drive a short low glitch on `rx_data` shorter than half a bit period (< 43 `clk` cycles) so `rx_data` returns to 1 before mid-bit check in `s_RX_START_BIT`. | FSM detects `r_Rx_Data != 1'b0` at `r_Clock_Count == (CLKS_PER_BIT-1)/2`, aborts back to `s_IDLE`, and keeps `o_valid` stable at `1'b0`. | `receiver.v` `s_RX_START_BIT` mid-bit check + SVA verifying no `o_valid` pulse on aborted start bit | FSM returns to `s_IDLE`; `o_valid` remains `1'b0` with no false pulse. | `uart_err_test` |
